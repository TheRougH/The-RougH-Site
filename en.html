THE BAND

Five musicians, one collective identity

The RougH is a French metal band made up of five musicians brought together by the same drive: to play powerful, direct and uncompromising music.

On Let Them Die, music, lyrics and arrangements are collectively credited to The RougH. The record blends a distinctly metal energy with deeply human themes.
